Notepad & Editor
Multi-Tab Workspace: Open and edit multiple notes simultaneously. Unsaved changes display a subtle status indicator on the tab.
Typewriter & Focus Mode: Toggle with Ctrl + Shift + F to keep your active line centered and dim surrounding text while writing.
Search & Replace: Press Ctrl + F to trigger search with match counts, case sensitivity, and whole-word options.
Live Status Bar: Track line and column numbers, total character counts, word counts, and live selection details.
Version History & Checkpoints
Automated Snapshots: Bilet continuously creates background snapshots as you write without interrupting your workflow.
Custom Milestone Labels: Tag key version revisions with custom labels for easy timeline navigation.
Restore & Diff: Open Version History to compare revisions side-by-side (Diff) or restore previous notes instantly.
Security & Vault Protection
Master Password Encryption: Your notes are protected with local master password AES encryption.
Auto-Lock Timer: Configure an idle timeout in Settings to lock your vault automatically when away.
Backup & Export: Export your vault content directly from Security settings for offline backups.
History & Trash Recovery
Safe Deletion: Closed note tabs are archived in History/Trash rather than permanently deleted.
Trash Inspection & Restore: Review deleted notes in History and restore them to your active workspace anytime.
Personalization & Fonts
Custom Font Upload: Import .ttf or .otf font files directly into Bilet.
Theme & Typography: Toggle Light/Dark modes and adjust font sizes for comfortable viewing.
